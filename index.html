The Bookshelf

Illustrated novels, series walks, comics, picture books and field guides — every reading thing on Sean Makes Stuff, in one bookcase. The Scriptorium books start as a short brief and come out as a readable novel, a printable PDF and a narrated audiobook, and each lists its prompts, tokens, cost and timing so you can see the seams. Take one down and have a look.

AI-authored

Everything on these shelves was written by AI models, and the pictures and voices are AI too. Each book carries a sticker and lists who made what. The one exception to the writing is The Sleeper Awakes: that’s H. G. Wells’s own text, and AI only illustrated and narrated it.

The Sleeper Awakes

made in a day — Sep 25, 2026

A Victorian insomniac falls into a trance in 1897 and wakes in 2100 to find that his untouched fortune has grown until he owns half the world — and that he has become a legend to a city of moving roads, flying machines and a vast blue-clad labouring class. The first shelf book that isn't ours: Wells's public-domain text, read verbatim, with every chapter illustrated as a Franco-Belgian comic album and narrated by a fourteen-voice cast rendered locally.
