The Bookshelf

Illustrated books made end-to-end with LLMs, image models, and text-to-speech. Each one starts as a short brief and exits as a readable novel, a printable PDF, and a narrated audiobook — typically in under an hour of wall-clock time. Every project lists its prompts, tokens, cost, and timing so you can see the seams. Take one down and have a look.
