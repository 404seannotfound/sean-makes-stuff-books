The text

The Sleeper Awakes by H. G. Wells (1866–1946) is the author’s 1910 revision of When the Sleeper Wakes, first serialised in The Graphic and published as a book in 1899. The work is in the public domain in the United States (published before 1930) and in countries where copyright lasts for the author’s life plus 70 years or less (Wells died in 1946).

Illustrations

The illustrations are new to this edition (2026). They were generated with an AI image model (xAI Grok Imagine) from scene descriptions drawn from Wells’s text, in a Franco-Belgian comic-album style of ink linework with watercolour. They are original images and do not reproduce the work of any other illustrator.

Narration

The audiobook is new to this edition (2026). It is a multi-voice performance generated on our own hardware with the open-weight Kokoro-82M text-to-speech model (Apache 2.0 licence), using its built-in voices: am_adam, am_echo, am_eric, am_fenrir, am_michael, am_onyx, am_puck, am_santa, bf_emma, bf_isabella, bm_daniel, bm_fable, bm_george, bm_lewis. The narration reads Wells’s chapters verbatim. It does not narrate the preface, the table of contents, or any Project Gutenberg material. Word-by-word read-along timing comes from the model’s own timestamps.
