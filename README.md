# Sean Makes Stuff — Books

The books shelf of [sean-makes-stuff.com](https://sean-makes-stuff.com), split into its
own GitHub Pages site (books.sean-makes-stuff.com) because the audiobooks pushed the main
site past the 1GB Pages limit. Five shelves: novels & read-alongs, series walks, comics, picture
books & fables, and tutorials & field guides. The Scriptorium books carry their own storyboard art and
multi-voice audiobooks; the comics, walks and field guide live on sean-makes-stuff.com or their own
pages.dev sites and are shelved here with covers in `covers/`. Every book is labeled for what AI made
(sticker on the cover, Words/Art/Voices credits in its examine dialog). The make-series-notes skill
shelves new walks under Series walks automatically.
